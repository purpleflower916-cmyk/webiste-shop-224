How to request a record

Step 1
Complete the request form for a private body (Form C), which is on the Information Regulator’s website, and email it to shop224.info@gmail.com. Tell us who you are, give proof of your identity, name the record, say how you want to receive it, and say which right you are protecting. If you ask for someone else, include proof that you may act for them.
Step 2
We decide within 30 days. We may extend this by up to 30 days and will tell you why. If we do not reply in time, PAIA treats this as a refusal.
Step 3
If we agree, we tell you any fee and how you will get the record.
Fees
PAIA’s regulations set the request fee, the fee for searching for and preparing a record, and the copying fees. A person asking for their own personal information does not pay the request fee. We tell you the amount first, and may ask for a deposit if finding the record takes a long time.
If we refuse
We may only refuse on the grounds PAIA allows, for example to protect another person’s personal information or confidential business information. We give our reasons in writing. You may then complain to the Information Regulator or apply to a court.

Personal information we process

Purposes
Making and delivering orders, contacting customers, handling returns, keeping legal and financial records, answering questions, and publishing reviews with permission.

Names, phone numbers, shipping addresses, email addresses, order details and messages, about customers, people who contact us and website visitors.

Our delivery partner, our payment provider, and our website host and tools. The authorities, where the law requires.
Outside South Africa
Some providers may store information outside South Africa. We choose providers bound by law or agreement to protect it to a standard similar to POPIA.
