At Shop224, we believe that jewellery should be more than just a beautiful accessory—it should be a tangible canvas for your most cherished memories, milestones, and personal journeys.

"We do not mass-produce. We engrave to order with the exact words, names, and coordinate markers that hold a permanent space in your heart."

Our Craft & Process

Operating locally in South Africa, we specialize in sleek, minimalist custom-engraved pieces. Every initial necklace, classic date band ring, monogram bracelet, and initial drop earring in our collection is treated as an individual piece of art. When you submit your personalization details through our store, our production process begins crafting a piece exclusive to you.

Our Commitment to Quality

Because bespoke jewellery carries profound emotional weight, we use durable, hypoallergenic base metals finished with clean precious metal platings. Whether you select our brilliant crisp Silver or our classic luminous Yellow Gold finish, your piece is designed to serve as a long-lasting keepsake of the moments that define you.
