About Shop224

Made for one person at a time.

Shop224 makes personalised jewellery. You choose the piece and the metal, type the letters or words that matter, and we add them to your piece.

What we make

Four pieces, each personalised with your own text. Gold pieces are 14K to 18K PVD gold plated stainless steel. Silver pieces are stainless steel.

Initial Necklace

A necklace carrying the letter or letters of your choice, up to three.

Letter Bracelet

A bracelet that spells out a name or a word, one letter at a time.

Letter Necklace

A necklace that spells out a name or a word, one letter at a time.

Stud Earrings

Round studs with a letter on each. Type two for a pair, or one for a match.

How it works

From choosing a piece to having it made takes four steps.

Choose a piece

Pick a necklace, bracelet or earrings, then choose gold or silver.

Enter your text

Type initials, a name or a word in the Enter Text box. Each piece shows how many characters it takes.

Add it to your bag

Check the spelling, then add the piece to your bag.

We make it

Your piece is personalised to order and sent to you. Shipping & Returns

Why we personalise

Most jewellery gifts are guesses. Personalising removes the guess. A piece with a name, an initial or a short message on it is a wearable memory, a mark of who someone is, or a way to celebrate a moment that matters.

That is why every piece in the collection carries your text. The wearer feels seen, and the piece cannot be mistaken for anyone else's.
